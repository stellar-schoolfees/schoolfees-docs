These observations are not acceptance criteria or a guarantee. The [terms](https://docs.drips.network/wave/terms-and-rules/)
leave selection to the organizers. The [application limits](https://docs.drips.network/wave/maintainers/repo-application-limits/)
may apply per person across organizations and per organization; creating more
organizations does not create more personal slots. Check remaining slots in the application flow.

The [official wave page](https://www.drips.network/wave/stellar) currently lists
Wave 10 for October 15–22. Its displayed times do not establish an application
cutoff or timezone. October 9 remains a user-supplied preparation target; confirm
the actual application closing timestamp through Drips before relying on it.

For this window, SchoolFees, EventBadges and InvoicePay contracts have distinct
audiences and implemented workflows. Ajo and DuesTreasury remain governance
scaffolds, and InvoicePay's frontend is incomplete. Prioritize complete,
reproducible repositories and genuine contributor issues over repository count.
